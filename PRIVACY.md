# Self Attune — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

Self Attune is a conversation-coaching app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This
policy is short because the app collects almost nothing.

## What stays on your device

**Audio is never uploaded.** Self Attune listens to conversations through your
iPhone's microphone and analyses them entirely on the device. There is no
setting that changes this — no server ever receives your audio.

**Transcription is on-device.** Speech recognition uses Apple's on-device
recogniser (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition`). Your speech is not sent to Apple or
to us for transcription.

**Recordings are local.** If a conversation is recorded, the audio file is
written to the app's private storage on your iPhone and never leaves it.
Deleting a conversation in the app deletes the file.

**Your voice profile is local.** The 30-second enrollment produces a small set
of numbers describing your voice — loudness, pitch and timbre averages. It is
stored on the device and is not transmitted. It is not a recording and cannot be
played back or used to reconstruct your voice.

## What can be uploaded, only if you turn it on

Metrics sync is **off by default**. If you enable "Upload metrics" in Settings,
the app sends numeric session data — talk share, speaking rate, loudness
differences, cue timings — to a private database controlled by the developer.
This contains no audio and no transcript text.

## What we never collect

No name, email, address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising
identifier, or device identifier. No analytics SDK. No third-party trackers. No
advertising. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

## Other people in your conversations

Self Attune analyses the other person's speech in order to compare your pace and
volume to theirs. That analysis happens on your device and is not stored as
anything identifying them. **Recording laws vary.** Some places require every
participant to consent before a conversation is recorded. You are responsible
for knowing and following the law where you are.

## HealthKit

Self Attune requests workout permission for one reason: starting a workout
session is the only way iOS can launch the Apple Watch app for you, and it is
what keeps the Watch app running for a full conversation. No health data is
read, stored, or transmitted, and none is used for advertising or shared with
third parties.

## Your control

Delete any conversation from the History tab. Re-record or clear your voice
profile in Settings. Deleting the app removes all local data, including
recordings and your voice profile.
