# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 September 2026_

Self Attune is built so that the sensitive thing it handles — the sound of your
conversations — never leaves the device it was recorded on.

## What stays on your iPhone

**Audio.** Self Attune records the conversation to your iPhone so you can listen
back to it later. That recording is written to the app's private storage on the
device. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never sent to anyone else.
Deleting a conversation in the app deletes the audio with it.

**Transcripts.** Speech recognition runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's
on-device recogniser. Self Attune explicitly requires on-device recognition, so
your speech is not sent to Apple's servers for transcription. Transcripts are
stored on the device alongside the recording.

**Your voice profile.** During setup you read a short passage so the app can
tell your voice apart from the person you are talking to. The result is a
handful of numbers describing your voice — loudness, pitch and timbre averages.
No audio from that recording is kept. The profile stays on the device.

## What we collect

Nothing, by default. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK.

If you switch **Upload metrics** on in Settings, the app sends conversation
*numbers* — duration, talk share, pace, how many cues fired and whether you
corrected — to a private backend operated by the app's developer. Audio,
transcripts and your voice profile are never included in that upload under any
setting. The toggle is off unless you turn it on, and turning it off stops all
uploading.

## Other people in the conversation

Self Attune records audio that will usually include people other than you. Laws
about recording conversations differ by jurisdiction, and in some places every
participant must consent. You are responsible for complying with the law where
you are.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — to hear the conversation. This is the app's core function.
- **Speech recognition** — to produce the transcript, on-device only.
- **Health (workout session)** — used solely so your iPhone can wake the Apple
  Watch app and keep it running for the length of a conversation. Self Attune
  does not read your health data and writes only the mindfulness session it
  starts for this purpose.

## Children

Self Attune is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
information from them.

## Your control

Every conversation can be deleted from the History screen, which removes the
audio, the transcript and the metrics for it. Deleting the app removes
everything it stored.
